FAQ

Questions people ask first.

If yours isn’t here, call or email and we’ll answer it.

Is membership health insurance?

No. Membership does not replace your health insurance. Your plan is still used for scans, tests and referrals to other specialists. Surgery, therapy, additional orthotics and outside care are billed separately or through insurance.

Do I need a referral?

No. Membership is by referral or inquiry. You can call or email us yourself, or ask your doctor or financial advisor to introduce you.

How quickly can I be seen?

Members get same-day or next-day visits when available, and can message Dr. Shar directly. Black members also have after-hours and weekend access.

Does Dr. Shar perform surgery?

Yes. She is a foot and ankle surgeon. When surgery is the right answer, members have the same surgeon from the first exam through recovery, with 90 days of check-ins afterward.

What is the Mobility Exam?

A yearly check of how you walk, your strength, balance and shoes, with a written plan and custom-made orthotics tailored to how you move.

Can my family join?

Yes. Family members can join at a lower rate.
